Dentistry Clinic

Transforming a trusted local clinic into a modern, trust-driven digital experience that increases appointment bookings.

Key Design Decisions

Introduced a calm and consistent colour palette to reduce visual noise
Designed a strong hero section to immediately communicate brand and trust
Used real patient imagery and testimonials to build credibility
Implemented large, clickable service cards for easy navigation
Added sticky CTAs to support quick booking
Focused on mobile-first responsiveness throughout

Impact & Learnings

Patients can now find and book appointments faster through clear, visible CTAs

Reduced basic inquiry calls by making services and contact information easier to access

Built stronger trust with new patients through real testimonials and doctor presence

Created a smoother mobile booking journey for busy patients searching on the go

"Healthcare websites need to feel clear, calm, and trustworthy before they feel creative. Small UX decisions — CTA placement, content hierarchy, patient-focused messaging — make a dental website feel more approachable."
