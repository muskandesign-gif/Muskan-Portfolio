The Person Behind the Work
I'm Muskan Shah, a designer, creative thinker, and storyteller passionate about turning ideas into meaningful experiences. My journey in design began with a background in product design and later evolved into design innovation and brand management during my master's in London. Spending two years there shaped the way I see creativity, culture, and human - centered design.Today, I bring that blend of strategy and creativity into everything I create — from UI/UX and visual design to brand storytelling and digital experiences. I love designing work that feels intuitive, engaging, and human because, to me, good design isn't just about visuals, it's about creating something people genuinely connect with.
